    How to Play

    🎯 Goal

    Reach the swirling portal at the end of each level. There are 15 levels, each harder than the last, ending with the final boss.

    🔄 Flip gravity

    Blink (webcam mode) or press your Flip key to swap between walking on the floor and the ceiling. You can only flip while standing on a surface, so plan ahead!

    ⌨️ Controls

    🎮 Gamepad & 👆 Touch

    Gamepad: stick / D-pad to move, any face button or bumper to flip, Start to pause.
    Phones & tablets: ◀ ▶ buttons on the left, big ⇅ button on the right.

    👁️ Webcam tips

    Good front light, face the camera, about an arm's length away. Calibrate with eyes open. If blinks are missed, raise Blink sensitivity in Settings; if it flips by itself, lower it. Keyboard works at the same time.

    ☠ Hazards

    • Spikes — don't touch
    • Saws — some move
    • Pits — falling out = death
    • Cracked blocks — break after 0.5s
    • Purple zone — you can't flip inside
    • Lasers — flicker before turning on
    • The Void — chases you, keep moving
    • Liar Mode — screen flashes pink, then gravity flips by itself

    👹 Final boss

    The Grand Liar fires rings of bullets. Touch the glowing yellow orbs (6 total) to hurt it. Every 2 hits it gets angrier: saws, then lasers, then Liar Mode.

    👥 Multiplayer

    Local: 1–4 players on one device with separate keys or gamepads.
    Online: host a room, share the 5-letter code, friends join from any device.
    Checkpoints are shared. First to the portal wins the round; others get 5 seconds to finish. Players who fall far behind are pulled to the leader.

    Settings

    Click a key to change it, then press the new key. Esc clears it.
